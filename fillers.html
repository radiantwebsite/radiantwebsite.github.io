Dermal Fillers

Revanesse dermal fillers replace lost collagen - smoothing folds and naturally enhancing lips, cheeks and chin.

Why dermal fillers?

The skin on our face is supported by collagen. As we age, the collagen thins and wrinkles appear; lifestyle, genetics and gravity also weaken the collagen fibres. Dermal fillers act as collagen replacement, helping the skin regain its youthful and natural appearance.

We use the Revanesse brand - non-permanent and manufactured in North America. Revanesse is the only dermal filler with the added benefits of keeping skin well hydrated and stimulating skin regeneration. It is very safe and non-allergenic, as it contains no animal products.

Conditions fillers can help

Fillers are injected just below the surface of the skin, filling in undesired folds and wrinkles - most commonly the lines beside the nose, on the forehead and around the mouth.

They can also enhance shape and lushness for thin lips, and we use fillers to augment hollows in the cheeks and the chin.

The procedure

We first provide a unique face cleanser that cleans without dilating your pores, and the doctor removes all impurities with an antiseptic wipe.

The skin is then anaesthetised with a topical cream, and the filler is given with a tiny needle exactly where enhancement is desired. The doctor uses as much filler as necessary to flatten wrinkles and deep folds, always aiming for a natural, healthy look. The whole procedure takes about 10 to 20 minutes.

Afterwards, keep your face relaxed and avoid heat treatments. We review you at 2 weeks, and a touch-up is done free of charge until you are completely satisfied. More information: revanesse.com.

  • 10-20 minutes
  • Non-permanent, non-animal filler
  • Hydrates & regenerates skin
  • Free 2-week review & touch-up
