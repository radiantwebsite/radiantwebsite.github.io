Mesotherapy

A non-surgical anti-cellulite and contouring programme that melts trapped fat and smooths dimpled skin.

What is mesotherapy?

Our skin loses vital nutrients and develops cellulite as a result of hormonal changes, poor diet, natural aging and genetics. Cellulite is caused by connective tissue bands that hold down the skin, trapping fat and causing a "dimpling" or orange-peel appearance - unsightly and especially obvious on the buttocks, thighs and tummy, and it does not go away easily even with exercise.

Mesotherapy is a non-surgical anti-cellulite and contouring programme which infuses the skin with a blend of multi-vitamins and fat metabolisers. The infusion destroys the connective tissue bands and melts the trapped fat, which is then eliminated from the body naturally, creating a smooth, dimple-free appearance.

What can mesotherapy help?

Mesotherapy treats cellulite effectively and improves the appearance of cellulite-prone skin. It is also effective for spot fat reduction and is anti-aging - resulting in firmer, smoother skin with reduced open pores and fine wrinkles, and a clearer complexion.

It can be applied to the tummy, buttocks, thighs, face, neck, arms and eye area.

The treatment

We begin with a gentle cleansing and exfoliation to ensure maximum absorption. A topical anaesthetic can be applied if necessary. The treatment is then applied to the target areas with a specially manufactured micro-needle that delivers the medicine in a series of painless micro-injections.

The process takes 20-45 minutes depending on the site. Mild bruising may occur and fades within days; otherwise there is no restriction on activities. A warm sensation may be felt as the fats are metabolised, and an easy-to-follow healthy diet is advised to circulate the solution and flush out toxins.

Results can be seen within a week, with further improvement over the next two months, and can last for years depending on lifestyle and eating habits. For best results we suggest at least 3-5 sessions, each spaced one month apart.

  • 20-45 minutes per session
  • Non-surgical
  • Results within 1 week
  • Recommended: 3-5 sessions, 1 month apart
